Lead Automation Engine

A transparent record prepared for the planned Ops project. The sections are ready; project evidence will replace clearly marked placeholders as work progresses.

01 — Project context

What problem are we solving?

Evidence needed

Document the real intake process, actors, pain points, constraints and success criteria before implementation claims are added.

02 — Human understanding

Say it without node names.

The Ops concept describes a system that receives inbound leads, checks their quality, enriches and scores them, stores the result, and alerts the appropriate person. The precise business rules remain to be documented.

03 — Initial hypothesis

The first mental model.

Webhook→Validate→Enrich→Score→Database→Notify
Source boundary: this sequence comes from the Ops project registry. It is architecture intent, not evidence of a built or tested workflow.

04 — Prompt engineering

What language changed the design?

Original and revised prompts needed

Add the prompt, intended behaviour, weak output, diagnosis, revision and resulting implementation change. Prompt text should earn its place by showing causality.

05 — Workflow architecture

Nodes with reasons, not decoration.

For each confirmed stage, explain its input, output, dependencies and failure behaviour. Relevant guide concepts can link directly to Triggers, data shaping and reliability.

06–09 — Break, diagnose, fix

Failures only when they happened.

No failures supplied yet

Record the observed symptom, actual cause, evidence used during diagnosis, and the smallest effective fix. Never invent a dramatic failure to make the story interesting.

10 — Testing

Prove the important paths.

Test evidence needed

Add happy path, invalid input, duplicate submission, external-service failure and edge-case results when they have actually been run.

11 — Production thinking

What could hurt on a bad day?

Future evidence should address idempotency, credentials, retries, error workflows, logging, auditability and observability in proportion to the system’s actual risks.

12–14 — Final design and lessons

Close the loop when the build earns it.

Awaiting implementation

The final architecture, lessons and repository/workflow evidence will be published only after they exist.
